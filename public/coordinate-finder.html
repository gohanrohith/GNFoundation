Certificate Coordinate Finder

How to use:

  1. Upload your certificate PDF below
  2. Click on the PDF where you want each field to appear
  3. The coordinates will be displayed and saved automatically
  4. Copy the coordinates and paste them in the Admin Panel's "Configure Fields" dialog

Note: PDF coordinates start from BOTTOM-LEFT corner (0,0). Higher Y values = move UP.

Click "Start" to begin placing fields. Current field: Admission Number
