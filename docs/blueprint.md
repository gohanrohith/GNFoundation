# **App Name**: GNF Website

## Core Features:

- Homepage: Landing page with an introduction to the GN Foundation, its mission, and key initiatives.
- About GN: A dedicated page detailing the story of Girrem Narayanamma and the foundation's origin.
- RMTH/RSTH Info: Information page about the Regional Mathematics/Science Talent Hunt exam.
- Participation Page: A page for students to enter their admission number.
- Certificate Download: Allow students to download their certificates by confirming their admission number. Access is gated by the result of lookup into the database. This is a necessary security precaution.
- Awards & Prizes: Showcase past awardees and the prizes/certificates they received to motivate future participants.
- Contact Information: Provide contact details and a contact form for inquiries.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep blue (#1E3A8A), inspired by the pursuit of knowledge and trust, for headers and key elements.
- Background color: Light blue (#E3F2FD), a softer tint of the primary to create a welcoming and calm environment.
- Accent color: Golden yellow (#FFC107), reminiscent of awards and achievements, for highlights and calls to action.
- Body text and headlines: 'PT Sans' to blend warmth and modernity for readable content.
- Use clear, modern icons representing education, talent, and awards.
- Maintain a clean and intuitive layout for easy navigation and a user-friendly experience.
- Use subtle animations to enhance user engagement without being distracting.
